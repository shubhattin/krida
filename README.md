# पदावली (Padavali) 🎯

A fun, interactive Sanskrit word puzzle game that tests your creativity, expands your vocabulary, and lets you challenge your friends to beat your score!

## ✨ Features

- **Multi-Script Support**: Play in 6 different Indian scripts:
  - Devanagari (देवनागरी)
  - Telugu (తెలుగు)
  - Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ)
  - Gujarati (ગુજરાતી)
  - Bengali (বাংলা)
  - Odia (ଓଡ଼ିଆ)

- **Interactive Grid**: Click and drag to find hidden words
- **Real-time Transliteration**: Seamlessly switch between scripts
- **Modern UI**: Beautiful, responsive design with dark theme support
- **Progressive Web App**: Install and play directly
