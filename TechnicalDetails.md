## 🚀 Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: Next.js 15 with React 19
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS and Shadcn UI
- **Database**: PostgreSQL with Drizzle ORM (Neon)
- **Authentication**: Better Auth
- **State Management**: Jotai
- **API**: tRPC for type-safe APIs
- **Animations**: Framer Motion
- **Deployment**: Netlify/Vercel (Edge Runtime)

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Select Your Script**: Choose from 6 supported Indian scripts
2. **Find Words**: Look for hidden words in the grid
3. **Click & Drag**: Select words by clicking and dragging across letters
4. **Score Points**: Find all words to complete the puzzle
5. **Challenge Friends**: Share your score and challenge others!

## 🌐 Multi-Language Support

Padavali uses a sophisticated transliteration engine (`lipi_lekhika`) that converts Sanskrit text between different Indian scripts in real-time. This allows users to:

- Play the same puzzle in their preferred script
- Learn how Sanskrit words appear in different writing systems
- Appreciate the linguistic diversity of India
